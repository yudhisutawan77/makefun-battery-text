One line of at most 200 characters: the phone battery's final message to
the charger it waited for all day.

## Entry

> I waited all day at 1% while you charged everything but me: the watch, the earbuds, the vape. Enjoy the dark. Your alarm dies with me.

134 characters, one line.

## How it works

`main.py` prints the entry text exactly once to stdout and writes it to
`/output/entry.txt` (plus `$ENTRY_OUT` / `$ENTRY_OUT_DIR` when set), which
is the artifact the house gate scorer reads.
